**New**
- **Real trader models and voices.** Seven traders (Prapor, Therapist, Skier, Mechanic, Ragman, Jaeger, Fence) can stand in their rooms as their real models and say a line when you start to trade. The mod does not contain them: it reads them from your own installed copy of the 1.0 game. Usually there is nothing to set up; if they do not show, fill in **F12 → Trader models → Folder of the 1.0 game**. Without that game nothing changes.
- **A guide at start.** Ten short steps over the main menu: where the first trader is, trading, the hideout, building, crafting, repairs, trader models, and a choice of what a death takes. "Don't show again" switches it off; **Guide** in the main menu opens it again.
- **Repair kits work in a raid.** Right-click a weapon or armour piece and choose Repair. Kits you carry count, and those in the stash while you are in the hideout. Weapons need the Workbench built, armour the Lavatory.
- **Sell what you wear.** Slung and holstered weapons, helmet, headset and armour are in the trader's sell list and come off you when sold. A pack, rig or case is only offered when empty; the secure container never; the weapon in your hands is left out.
- **Sort stash** on the Stash station.
- New setting to switch off Vagabond's own hideout exits (on by default).

**Fixes**
- Coming into the hideout you now stand just inside the door you will leave by, not in the middle of the hall.
- Going through the Security door no longer leaves you facing a wall or looking through the level.
- In the hideout the map's wind, birds and far-off shots are no longer heard, the game's Hideout volume setting applies, and bots that spawn while you are inside are frozen within a second.
- Items made for the special slots (a compass, for one) fell through the trader's table and were lost. They now stay on it.
- Ammunition, money or food taken from a trader's table could be handed out again by "Collect deliveries".
- The door out of a trader's room read "Left"; it now reads "Leave the room".
- With Dynamic Maps, a trader's door could be missing from the map.

**Changed**
- The middle choice of **When you die** has a new name, `LoseCarriedGear`. If you had chosen it, choose it again (F12 → Death, or in the guide).

Requires SPT 4.1.6 and Vagabond 0.8.3+. Not compatible with Fika.
